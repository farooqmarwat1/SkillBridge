How it works

From request to crew on site

Five steps, one coordinator from start to finish.

  1. 1

    Request

    Send roles, headcount, site and dates by form, phone or WhatsApp.

  2. 2

    Shortlist

    We match candidates and send CVs with certificates and the quote.

  3. 3

    Approve

    You review CVs, interview if needed, and confirm the crew.

  4. 4

    Mobilise

    Induction, PPE and transport, with gate documents ready.

  5. 5

    Support

    Timesheets, replacements and extensions through one coordinator.

Gate-ready paperwork

Documents that arrive with each worker

Your site gate and HSE team get what they ask for, before day one. Final list to confirm with Skill Bridge.

  • Iqama and passport copies
  • CV and trade certificates
  • Medical fitness certificate
  • HSE induction record
  • TÜV cards for scaffolders and riggers
  • Operator licences for crane and forklift crews
