Who we are

An integrated partner for industrial sites

Skill Bridge is an efficient provider of human resources and equipment for industry and general contracting in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

Our team of goal-oriented professionals delivers techno-skilled manpower services and diversified support to the oil, gas and petrochemical industries, including information technology support services. We bring quality services to our clients, with value-added inputs and suggestions on every job.
